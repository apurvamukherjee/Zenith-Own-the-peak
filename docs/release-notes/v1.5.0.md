A faster app, and tips that stay gone.

- **Faster everywhere.** Logging a set, water or a meal does far less background work, which you'll notice most after weeks of history. The cloud-sync code only loads if you've set up sync.
- **Tips that stay dismissed.** The first-visit tips on Train, Nutrition and the Planner used to come back every time you opened the page. Dismiss one now and it's gone for good. They also sit next to the thing they explain.
- **Quick-log button out of the way.** It shrinks into a slim tab on the screen edge while you scroll, so it never covers a set's ✓ again.
- **PRs stay honest.** Deleting or editing a set re-checks every set after it, so the PR badges, PR count and Hall of Frame stay correct.
- **Fixes.** Two fast taps on your first set no longer create two sessions for the day. Water no longer says "Way behind" when you're a glass short right after waking. The focus timer keeps real time while your phone is locked.

Install: download `Zenith-v1.5.0.apk` on your phone and open it. It installs over v1.4.0 and keeps your data.
